02

Four hectares changes everything

Up to four hectares, one coordinate pair does the job. Past that the regulation wants the whole boundary. Getting this wrong is expensive precisely because you only find out late, and by then somebody has to walk the plot again.

It is also the fault our diagnostic turns up most often, usually in files that look complete at a glance.

03

Your origin country decides your obligation

Not your own country. This is the point most operators get wrong, in both directions.

A fit

  • You are the operator, first placing goods on the EU market
  • Medium or large, so December 2026 applies
  • Coffee, cocoa, palm oil, soya or timber
  • Smallholder or cooperative supply, where data arrives fragmented
  • No geospatial function in-house

Not a fit

  • Cattle hides, leather and vulcanised rubber, all removed from Annex I in 2026
  • Organisations with an established GIS team
  • Anyone wanting software to run themselves. Good ones exist and we will name them
  • Anyone wanting a guarantee of deforestation-free, or their declaration filed for them. We do neither.
